## Design Context

### Users
Puffin is for Android audiobook listeners who manage and play their own local library. They need dependable, low-friction playback, clear progress, and useful controls without accounts, ads, or tracking.

### Brand Personality
Calm, focused, and quietly rewarding. Puffin should feel minimal and trustworthy while giving listening progress enough personality to feel motivating.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use Puffin's established light and dark themes, blue accent, compact typography, Mage icons, restrained surfaces, and the supplied product references. Keep screens content-first and avoid marketing layouts, decorative gradients, nested cards, and visual effects that compete with book artwork.

### Design Principles
- Keep common audiobook actions obvious and reachable with one hand.
- Use real library and playback data; never present invented activity.
- Preserve a quiet hierarchy with compact controls and deliberate spacing.
- Support light and dark themes, system insets, screen readers, and large text.
- Keep personal data local and make editable profile elements visibly interactive.
